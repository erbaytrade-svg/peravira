About Pera Vira

Pera Vira connects travellers coming to Istanbul with licensed local tourism transport companies and drivers.

We review each driver's operating licence and vehicle documents, keep one clear fixed price, and leave payment between you and your driver. Pera Vira is operated by ERBAYTRADE LTD, based in Istanbul.

Questions

Do I pay in the app?

No. You pay your driver directly. The app only connects you with a licensed driver.

Which city do you cover?

Istanbul and its airports.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. You see one daily price before you confirm, with no haggling at the airport.

How do I know it is my driver?

Your driver confirms the meeting with a code that you share, so only the right person takes you.

How long is a day, and how many kilometres?

A day is up to 12 hours and up to 100 km within Istanbul. Extra hours or distance are agreed with your driver in advance. The service covers Istanbul only.

Is this an on-demand taxi?

No. It is a reservation service: the driver and car are arranged in advance so they are ready when you arrive. Please book at least 24 hours ahead.
